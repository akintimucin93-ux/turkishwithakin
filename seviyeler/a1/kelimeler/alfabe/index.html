Türkçeye özgü harfler / Special letters

Ç, Ğ, I, İ, Ö, Ş, Ü harflerine dikkat et. I–ı noktasız; İ–i noktalıdır.

I–ı and İ–i are different letters. Ğ does not begin Turkish words: its example is dağ. “Harfi dinle” plays the letter name, not an isolated sound.
